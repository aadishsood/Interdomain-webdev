Automated robotic system / 2026

PAPR — Pick and Place Robot.

The Pick and Place Robot (PAPR) is an automated robotic system, which identifies, classifies, picks, and sorts objects into their respective designated locations. The system makes use of computer vision to analyze the shape, structure, and position of incoming objects and make decisions regarding their identification, and placement.

02 / components

Inside the
system.

Every module of PAPR, from seeing to gripping, works as one connected pipeline.

01

Camera & vision

Captures live views of incoming objects for shape, structure, and position analysis.

02

Sensors

Detect object presence and fine position along the pick and place path.

03

Robotic arm

Multi-axis arm that lifts, moves, and places each object at its target.

04

Gripper

End effector that adapts its grasp to different shapes and sizes.

05

Conveyor

Moves objects steadily through the vision and pick zones.

06

Controller

The brain that turns vision output into arm and gripper actions.
